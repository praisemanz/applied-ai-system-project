# Artist Profiles

## Nujabes
Nujabes was a Japanese producer who shaped the modern lo-fi hip hop sound. His productions combine jazz piano samples, warm bass, and laid-back drum breaks. Listeners who enjoy J Dilla, Tomppabeats, or the Samurai Champloo soundtrack typically respond well to similar tracks.

## Kavinsky
Kavinsky is a French producer whose synthwave records evoke 1980s film soundtracks. His sound is dark, driving, and cinematic. Listeners who like The Midnight, Com Truise, or the Drive movie soundtrack will recognize the same palette.

## Bon Iver
Bon Iver is an American indie folk project led by Justin Vernon. The early catalog leans on acoustic guitar and falsetto harmonies, while later work incorporates electronic textures. Recommended for fans of Sufjan Stevens, Fleet Foxes, and The National.

## Daft Punk
Daft Punk was a French electronic duo whose work spans filter house, disco-funk, and synth-driven pop. Their records reward listeners who want danceable energy without sacrificing melody. Comparable artists include Justice, Stardust, and Cassius.

## Erykah Badu
Erykah Badu is a foundational neo-soul artist known for jazz-inflected vocals and groove-centered arrangements. Her work pairs well with D'Angelo, Hiatus Kaiyote, and Jill Scott.

## Brian Eno
Brian Eno helped define ambient music. His releases prioritize texture, slow evolution, and quiet attention. Comparable artists include Stars of the Lid, Tim Hecker, and Harold Budd.

## Paramore
Paramore is an American pop punk and pop rock band with high-energy guitar work and strong vocal hooks. Fans typically also enjoy Fall Out Boy, Hayley Williams solo work, and All Time Low.

## Joao Gilberto
Joao Gilberto was a Brazilian singer and guitarist who helped invent bossa nova. His recordings emphasize quiet voice, nylon-string guitar, and subtle samba pulse. Adjacent listening includes Stan Getz, Astrud Gilberto, and Antonio Carlos Jobim.
