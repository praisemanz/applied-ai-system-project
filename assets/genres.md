# Genre Profiles

## Lo-fi Hip Hop
Lo-fi hip hop is a downtempo style built around mellow piano loops, jazzy chord progressions, and dusty drum samples. Energy stays low, tempos sit between 70 and 90 BPM, and the texture feels intentionally imperfect (vinyl crackle, tape hiss). It is widely used as background music for studying, reading, or working because the steady rhythm reduces distraction without demanding attention.

## Synthwave
Synthwave revives 1980s film and video game soundtracks with analog-style synthesizers, gated reverb drums, and arpeggiated basslines. Tempos are typically 95 to 115 BPM, the mood is cinematic and nostalgic, and tracks often feel like they were lifted from a neon-lit night drive. Strong overlap with retrowave and outrun styles.

## Indie Folk
Indie folk leans on acoustic guitar, layered vocal harmonies, and intimate songwriting. Energy is moderate, acousticness is high, and lyrics often explore introspective or pastoral themes. It is well suited to reflective listening, road trips, and quiet evenings.

## House
House is a four-on-the-floor dance genre rooted in Chicago club culture. Tempos cluster around 118 to 128 BPM, basslines are prominent, and tracks reward sustained energy. Sub-styles include deep house (warmer, jazzier), tech house (percussive), and progressive house (build-and-drop structure).

## Neo-Soul
Neo-soul fuses classic R&B vocals with hip hop drums, jazz harmony, and live instrumentation. Tempos vary widely (70 to 105 BPM), valence is moderate to high, and arrangements emphasize groove and vocal expression. Common uses include dinner playlists, late-evening listening, and emotive focus work.

## Ambient
Ambient music prioritizes texture and atmosphere over rhythm or melody. Tempos are slow or absent, energy is very low, and acousticness varies (synth pads, field recordings, processed strings). It is commonly used for meditation, sleep, deep reading, and long focus sessions.

## Pop Punk
Pop punk pairs distorted guitars with catchy vocal hooks and high tempos (typically 150 to 180 BPM). Energy is high, valence trends optimistic-but-conflicted, and lyrics often deal with growing up, friendship, and frustration. Useful for workouts, driving, and energy boosts.

## Bossa Nova
Bossa nova is a Brazilian style blending samba rhythms with cool jazz harmony. Tempos are gentle (around 100 to 130 BPM), acousticness is high (nylon-string guitar, light percussion), and valence is warm. Strong fit for cafe ambience, dinner parties, and relaxed afternoons.
