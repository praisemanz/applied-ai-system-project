[moods.md#0] Focus and Study
For focus work, prefer low energy, low vocal density, and steady rhythm. Lo-fi hip hop, ambient, and downtempo electronic are reliable defaults. Avoid sudden dynamic changes, heavy distortion, or emotionally charged lyrics, which pull attention away from the task. Target tempo range is roughly 70 to 100 BPM.

[genres.md#5] Ambient
Ambient music prioritizes texture and atmosphere over rhythm or melody. Tempos are slow or absent, energy is very low, and acousticness varies (synth pads, field recordings, processed strings). It is commonly used for meditation, sleep, deep reading, and long focus sessions.

[genres.md#4] Neo-Soul
Neo-soul fuses classic R&B vocals with hip hop drums, jazz harmony, and live instrumentation. Tempos vary widely (70 to 105 BPM), valence is moderate to high, and arrangements emphasize groove and vocal expression. Common uses include dinner playlists, late-evening listening, and emotive focus work.
